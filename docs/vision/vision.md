# Vision

TMB AI OS will become a modular, AI-agnostic, cross-platform Enterprise
Intelligence Platform that can operate in cloud, on-premises, local, and hybrid
environments.

## Long-term direction

The platform will provide:

- A Company Brain for organizational memory and knowledge
- An AI Gateway supporting cloud and local models
- Workflow and automation capabilities
- Human approval and explainable decisions
- A secure plugin and marketplace ecosystem
- Consistent experiences across web, desktop, mobile, and tablet
- Open APIs, SDKs, CLI tools, and event-driven integrations

## North Star

> Build the platform organizations can rely on for decades, not only the AI
> models available today.
